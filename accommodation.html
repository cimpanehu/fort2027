Accommodation

All accommodation will be arranged at the University Guest Houses. For fully supported participants, accommodation is complimentary. Participants who are not fully supported will be provided subsidised rates. All guest houses are within 900 metres from the lecture hall.

Meals

For fully sponsored participants, all meals (breakfast, lunch and dinner) will be arranged through guest house dining. Snack packs and refreshments for everyone will be delivered to the lecture hall during coffee breaks. Participants who are not sponsored can also choose to have meals arranged at a minimal cost at the guest house, allowing easy access to food without leaving campus.
