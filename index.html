Lectures

Teaching team and talk abstracts

Click on any lecture card to open or close the abstract.

Cluster structures on flag varieties and applications in particle physics

Lara Bossinger
Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México (UNAM)
lara@im.unam.mx
Lecture series · 5h
These lectures cover total positivity, an introduction to cluster algebras, cluster structures on partial flag varieties, realizations of configuration spaces in quantum field theory as partial flag varieties, and applications of cluster structures to scattering amplitudes.
Introductory course

Infinite-dimensional Lie algebras and their representations: an introduction through examples

Samuel Lopes
University of Porto
slopes@fc.up.pt
Lecture series · 5h
This course introduces structural features and aspects of the representation theory of infinite-dimensional Lie algebras through examples such as the Heisenberg Lie algebra, the Virasoro Lie algebra, Bosonic Fock space, the Boson-Fermion correspondence, and Lie algebras of infinite matrices including gl(infinity) and sl(infinity).
Introductory course

Homological methods in Representation Theory

Andrea Solotar
University of Buenos Aires
asolotar@dm.uba.ar
Lecture series · 5h
This course presents recent progress on Han's conjecture and introduces homological tools such as Hochschild cohomology and homology, global dimension, and triangulated and derived categories. It also discusses newer methods used in this area, in particular tau-Hochschild cohomology.
Introductory course

Representation theory for combinatorial algebras

Roozbeh Hazrat
Western Sydney University
r.hazrat@westernsydney.edu.au
Lecture series · 5h
The lectures focus on representation theory for combinatorial algebras arising from graph-type constructions, especially higher-rank graph analogues of Leavitt path algebras, together with their irreducible representations and links with symbolic dynamics.
Introductory course

Representation theory and knots

Véronique Bazier-Matte
Université Laval
veronique.bazier-matte.1@ulaval.ca
Lecture series · 5h
This course studies a connection between knot theory and cluster algebras via representation theory. It explains how knot and link diagrams give rise to cluster structures, relates these to Alexander polynomials and Kauffman states, and examines compatible cluster variables and symmetry phenomena.
Advanced course

Functor-category-theoretic methods in representation theory

Amit Kuber
Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur
askuber@iitk.ac.in
Lecture series · 5h
This course gives a gentle introduction to functor categories and their use in the representation theory of bound quivers. Topics include algebraically compact modules, Serre localisation, Krull-Gabriel dimension, duality, Ziegler spectrum, and definable categories.
Advanced course

Representation Theory of Groups

Pooja Singla
Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur
psingla@iitk.ac.in
Lecture series · 5h
These lectures begin with representations, group algebras, irreducibility, character theory, induction, restriction, Mackey theory, and Clifford theory. They then move to irreducible representations of GLn(Fq), including cuspidal and principal series representations, and finally to smooth admissible representations over p-adic fields, parabolic induction, and the Bernstein decomposition.
Advanced course
