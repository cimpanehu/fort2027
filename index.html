About the School

Flavors of Representation Theory (FoRT) is a two-week CIMPA School aimed at master's students, graduate students, and early-career researchers. It offers an introduction to key directions in modern representation theory through lectures, tutorials, collaborative problem sessions, and mentoring. The school builds on a series of preparatory initiatives, including IWLPA 2025, the AESIM School on Flavors of Representation Theory, and is part of a broader effort to develop a sustained representation theory community at NEHU, across the North-East of India, and in neighboring regions.

The programme features six lecture series covering a range of active research areas, including cluster theory, infinite-dimensional Lie algebras, homological methods, combinatorial algebras, knot-theoretic interactions, functor categories, and group representations. It is structured around four foundational minicourses and three advanced lecture series, allowing participants to build essential background while gaining exposure to current research themes.

Beyond lectures, the school emphasizes active learning through tutorial sessions, collaborative jigsaw activities, Young Researchers’ Sessions, mentoring meetings, and guided research discussions. These components are designed to deepen conceptual understanding, promote mathematical communication, and foster sustained interaction among participants, lecturers, and early-career researchers.
